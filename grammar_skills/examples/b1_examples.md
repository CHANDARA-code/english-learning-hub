## Contextual Grammar Passage: The Young Inventor from Battambang

**Passage Word Count:** 304 words  
**Grammar Focus:** Past vs Present Perfect, Modals, 1st/2nd Conditionals, Passives, Relative Clauses  

> **¶1** Dara, a nineteen-year-old engineering student from Battambang, **has always been fascinated** by agricultural technology. Ever since he was a young boy, he **used to watch** his parents struggle in their rice paddies whenever dry seasons arrived. In rural Cambodia, water irrigation **is considered** one of the greatest obstacles for local farmers. If farmers **do not have** reliable water pumps, their crops **will wither** under the blazing sun. 
>
> **¶2** Last year, Dara **decided to construct** a solar-powered irrigation pump **which could operate** without expensive gasoline. He gathered discarded plastic pipes, bicycle gears, and secondhand solar panels **that were donated** by a local non-profit organization. While he **was assembling** the prototype in his father's workshop, many neighbors doubted his ambition. They believed that solar machinery **was too complex for** a teenager to build. 
>
> **¶3** However, Dara did not give up. *"If I **were** easily discouraged, I **would never accomplish** anything worthwhile,"* Dara explained with a smile. After three months of trial and error, his solar pump **was successfully tested** in front of the entire community. The device pumped three hundred liters of water per minute directly from the canal into the dry fields.
>
> **¶4** Since that memorable day, Dara **has received** invitations from several technological institutes in Phnom Penh. Local farmers no longer **have to spend** large sums on fuel, because sunlight is completely free. Dara hopes **to manufacture** ten more pumps before the next planting season begins. If his project **receives** government funding, thousands of families across the province **will benefit** from clean, renewable irrigation. His achievement proves that young people **who possess** creativity and determination can solve real-world problems.

## Grammar Structures Highlighted in the Passage

| Sentence Extract from Text | Target Grammar Feature | Grammatical Explanation & Khmer Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"...has always been fascinated..."* | **Present Perfect Simple** | Experience continuing up to the present (ចាប់អារម្មណ៍តាំងពីក្មេងរហូតដល់បច្ចុប្បន្ន) |
| *"...is considered one of the greatest..."*| **Present Simple Passive** | Action/belief where the agent is general (*is + V3*) |
| *"...If farmers do not have..., will wither..."*| **First Conditional** | Real, likely future cause and effect (*If + Pres, will + V1*) |
| *"...While he was assembling..., doubted..."* | **Past Continuous + Past Simple**| Background continuous action interrupted by past simple |
| *"...If I were easily discouraged, would..."*| **Second Conditional** | Unreal hypothetical condition in the present (*If I were...*) |
| *"...young people who possess creativity..."* | **Defining Relative Clause** | Specifying which people (*who + relative clause*) |

   C. during  
   D. while  

2. The solar panels ________ by an international non-profit foundation last year.  
   A. were donated  
   B. was donated  
   C. are donating  
   D. donated  

3. If you ________ regular maintenance on the water pump, it will last for a decade.  
   A. will perform  
   B. performed  
   C. perform  
   D. are performing

   A. mustn't  
   B. don't have to  
   C. shouldn't to  
   D. can't to

   A. invent  
   B. invented  
   C. inventing  
   D. to invent

## Answer Key with Detailed Grammatical Explanations

### Part 1 Solutions
1. **Answer: B (since).** *Since* is used with a fixed starting point in time (*since he was born*), whereas *for* requires a duration of time.
2. **Answer: A (were donated).** The subject *solar panels* is plural, and the action happened in finished past time (*last year*), requiring plural past passive (*were + V3*).
3. **Answer: C (perform).** In First Conditional *if-clauses*, we use Present Simple (*perform*), never *will*.
4. **Answer: B (don't have to).** Farmers have no obligation to pay because energy is free. *Mustn't* means strictly forbidden.
5. **Answer: C (inventing).** After adjectives with prepositions (*interested in*), verbs must always take the gerund form (`-ing`).

### Part 2 Solutions
6. **Answer: to** (*hope + to-infinitive*: *hopes to study*).
7. **Answer: when** (Past continuous background action interrupted by past simple: *was working... when her brother brought*).
8. **Answer: seen** (Past perfect with *never*: *had never seen*).
9. **Answer: which** (Relative pronoun for an object in a non-defining clause surrounded by commas).
10. **Answer: would** (Second conditional result clause: *If their village had... life would be...*).

### Part 3 Solutions
11. **was repaired by** (Past simple passive: *was + V3 + by*).
12. **should speak** (Modal verb of advice + base verb).
13. **must not play / mustn't play** (Prohibition modal for forbidden actions).
14. **might visit** (Modal of future possibility + base verb).
15. **is faster than** (Comparative adjective form: *fast → faster than*).

### Part 4 Solutions
16. **Correction: Remove 'but'** (*Although he was very tired, he finished...* — Never combine *Although* and *but* in the same sentence).
17. **Correction: doesn't have** (After auxiliary *doesn't*, the verb reverts to the base form *have*).
18. **Correction: have lived / have been living** (An ongoing duration spanning past into present with *for six years* requires Present Perfect, not Present Continuous).
19. **Correction: who / that** (Relative pronoun for people must be *who* or *that*, never *which*).
20. **Correction: were** (*If I were you...* — Second conditional uses subjunctive *were* for advice/unreal conditions).
