## Section 1: Present Perfect Simple vs Continuous

*Complete the sentences using either the **Present Perfect Simple** or the **Present Perfect Continuous**.*

1. The detectives (interrogate) __________________ the prime suspect for over three hours, but he still refuses to confess.
2. Maya (write) __________________ five comprehensive research proposals since she joined the department.
3. Why are your hands covered in grease? — I (repair) __________________ the lawnmower.
4. I (know) __________________ Professor Higgins for twelve years; he is an exceptional mentor.
5. They (negotiate) __________________ the commercial merger all week, but they (not / reach) __________________ an agreement yet.
6. The global economy (experience) __________________ unprecedented inflationary pressure recently.

## Section 13: Cambridge B2 Key Word Transformations

*Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using between **TWO and FIVE words**, including the word given.*

**51. It is impossible that Oliver forgot the anniversary because I reminded him this morning.**  
**HAVE**  
Oliver ________________________________________ the anniversary because I reminded him this morning.

**52. 'You should not disclose the client's identity,' the lawyer warned his assistant.**  
**WARNED**  
The lawyer ________________________________________ the client's identity.

**53. We couldn't go sailing because the gale was exceptionally fierce.**  
**PREVENTED**  
The fierce gale ________________________________________ sailing.

**54. I really regret not taking my teacher's academic advice.**  
**WISH**  
I ________________________________________ my teacher's academic advice.

**55. A professional catering firm is preparing the banquet for the gala.**  
**HAVING**  
The organizers ________________________________________ by a professional catering firm.

## ✅ Answer Key with Analytical Annotations

### Section 1
1. **have been interrogating** (Focus on long ongoing duration and activity).
2. **has written** (Focus on completed quantity/results: *five proposals*).
3. **have been repairing** (Present physical evidence of a recent continuous activity).
4. **have known** (*Know* is a stative verb; cannot take continuous form).
5. **have been negotiating / have not reached** (Ongoing process throughout the week vs incomplete result).
6. **has been experiencing / has experienced** (Recent ongoing trend).

### Section 2
7. **had already consumed** (Completed prior to another past event: fire brigade's arrival).
8. **had left** (Left slides earlier at the hotel).
9. **had been running** (Past physical cause of being breathless).
10. **had synthesized / had been working** (Discovery completed after months of continuous research).
11. **were deliberating / was gathering** (Concurrent past continuous actions).

### Section 3
12. **will have constructed** (Action completed before deadline: *By November 2028*).
13. **will be conducting** (Action in progress during a specified future timeframe: *between two and four*).
14. **will have published** (Completed prior to future graduation).
15. **will be celebrating** (In progress at a specific future moment).
16. **have finished** (*Will you have finished... by tomorrow?*).

### Section 4
17. **must have fallen** (High certainty past deduction: *must have + V3*).
18. **cannot have committed / can't have committed** (Logical impossibility in the past).
19. **should have sent** (Past criticism of an unfulfilled obligation).
20. **might have left / could have left** (Past possibility).
21. **must be** (Present deduction based on empirical evidence of empty restaurant).

### Section 5
22. **had not reacted** (Third conditional if-clause requires Past Perfect).
23. **would be living** (Mixed conditional: Past unreal condition $\rightarrow$ Present result).
24. **would have apologized** (Mixed conditional: Permanent trait $\rightarrow$ Past specific action).
25. **would have reached** (Third conditional result clause).
26. **would not be** (Mixed conditional: Past action $\rightarrow$ Present state).

### Section 6
27. **I had not spent all my savings on that unreliable sports car.** (Past regret $\rightarrow$ *wish + Past Perfect*).
28. **I spoke fluent Spanish.** (Present wish $\rightarrow$ *if only + Past Simple*).
29. **would stop playing loud techno music at midnight.** (Annoyance $\rightarrow$ *wish + would*).
30. **repaired the potholes on Main Street.** (*It's high time + Subject + Past Simple*).

### Section 7
31. **It is believed that the legendary sunken city lies beneath the Aegean Sea.**  
    **The legendary sunken city is believed to lie beneath the Aegean Sea.**
32. **to have embezzled state revenues.** (Past action reported in the present: *is reported to have + V3*).
33. **to uncover further tombs in the valley.** (Future expectation: *are expected to + V1*).

### Section 8
34. **had solar panels installed on his roof** (Causative past: *had + obj + V3*).
35. **have the lecture halls repainted** (Future causative).
36. **had offensive graffiti spray-painted on the store's windows** (Involuntary causative experience).

### Section 9
37. **to which several sovereign nations agreed** (Preposition *to* fronted before *which*).
38. **to whom I dedicated my doctoral thesis** (Preposition *to* fronted before *whom*).
39. **of which met the safety criteria** (Quantifier + *of which*).

### Section 10
40. **Despite the severe economic recession, consumer spending remained resilient.** (or: *Despite the economy experiencing...*).
41. **Even though the surgical team worked twelve hours without a break, they completed the procedure.**
42. **Domestic flights have become cheaper, whereas international travel has skyrocketed in price.**

### Section 11
43. **to purchase** (Paused another action in order to buy).
44. **to lock** (Obligation to perform a future task).
45. **gazing** (Vivid memory of a past experience).
46. **replacing** (Experimenting with a new solution).
47. **to inform** (Formal formula for communicating bad news).

### Section 12
48. **am used to** (Accustomed to; familiar with).
49. **used to** (Past discontinued habit).
50. **get used to** (The progressive process of adapting).

### Section 13
51. **cannot have forgotten / can't have forgotten** (Deduction of past impossibility).
52. **warned his assistant not to disclose** (Reporting verb *warn + object + not to + V1*).
53. **prevented us from going** (*prevent + object + from + -ing*).
54. **wish I had taken** (Past regret formula).
55. **are having the banquet prepared** (Present continuous causative).
