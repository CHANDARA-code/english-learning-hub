## ✅ Answer Key with Masterclass Linguistic Commentary

### Section 1
1. **Beneath the crumbling crypt lay an ancient marble sarcophagus.** (Locative inversion with intransitive verb *lay*).
2. **Down through the narrow mountain pass swept a sudden, violent squall.**
3. **Far beyond the perimeter walls stretched a magnificent vista of snow-capped alpine peaks.**
4. **Out of the burning cathedral and into the misty streets fled the fugitive.**
5. **From the central balcony of the town hall hung the flag of the new republic.**

### Section 2
6. **So blatant was the magistrate's judicial corruption that even his political allies refused to defend him.**
7. **Such was her academic erudition that the visiting Oxford scholars were left speechless.**
8. **So catastrophic was the drought that three entire provinces were declared famine emergency zones.**
9. **Such was the public outrage that the Prime Minister resigned within twenty-four hours.**

### Section 3
10. **having failed** (Nominative absolute with perfect active participle).
11. **permitting** (Fixed present participle nominative absolute).
12. **shaking with suppressed indignation** (Nominative absolute expressing physical accompaniment).
13. **depleted** (Passive participial nominative absolute).

### Section 4
14. **through ideological confrontation** (Verb phrase ellipsis omitting *sought security*).
15. **did not** (Verb phrase ellipsis omitting *enthusiastically endorse the theoretical model*).
16. **so, we proceed; if not, we appeal** (Cohesive clausal substitution with *so / not*).

### Section 5
17. **no small / no minor** (Litotes: *no small achievement*).
18. **not without** (Litotes preposition: *not without apprehension*).
19. **not without / by no means without** (Litotes idiom: *not without foundation*).
20. **in no small** (Litotes collocation: *in no small part*).

### Section 6
21. **lest the foreign syndicate intercept** (Base subjunctive form following *lest*).
22. **Be they** (Concessive inverted subjunctive: *Be they ally or adversary*).
23. **Cost it** (Archaic concessive inversion: *Cost it what it may*).
24. **live** (Optative subjunctive base verb with singular subject: *Long live*).
25. **tear / should tear** (Subjunctive after *lest*).

### Section 7
26. **in accordance with which all biochemical research must be conducted**
27. **the economic ramifications of which are only now becoming clear**
28. **to the tenets of which very few modern theorists still subscribe**

### Section 8
29. **would have continued / had been acting** (Past counterfactual result + prior continuous past state in reported speech).
30. **had plundered / entered** (Action completed prior to historic exploration event).
31. **is ratified / will have been negotiating** (Present time clause + Future Perfect Continuous duration).

### Section 9
32. **No sooner had the prime minister announced the** (*No sooner had + S + V3 + than*).
33. **nothing could have prevented the company's** (*nothing could have prevented + Noun*).
34. **lest the negotiations (should) be undermined** (*lest + Subject + (should) be + V3*).
35. **Such was his eloquence** (*Such was + Noun Phrase + that*).
36. **was by no means lacking in / was not lacking in** (Litotes with *lacking*).

### Section 10
37. **Correction: Through the dense woodland rushed the wounded stag...** (Locative inversion does not use the dummy auxiliary *did*; the main verb *rushed* inverts directly).
38. **Correction: The delegates having finished the draft, the press conference was convened.** (Remove *and then*; a nominative absolute cannot be joined by coordinating conjunctions).
39. **Correction: Lest the ambassador be misunderstood... / Lest the ambassador should be misunderstood...** (*Lest* governs the base subjunctive *be*, not *should is*).
40. **Correction: So furiously did the hurricane rage that... / So furious was the hurricane that...** (*So* requires subject-auxiliary inversion).
41. **Correction: Into the dark cavern they ventured...** (Locative/directional inversion is prohibited when the subject is a pronoun; pronoun precedes verb: *they ventured*).
