## ✅ Answer Key with Full Explanations

### Section 1
1. **have lived** (*since 2018* indicates an action starting in the past and continuing to now).
2. **graduated** (*two years ago* indicates completed past time → Past Simple).
3. **Have you ever eaten** (*ever* with life experience questions → Present Perfect).
4. **didn't see / did not see** (*last weekend* specifies finished past time).
5. **has just left** (*just* with immediate past result connected to now).
6. **wrote** (Shakespeare is deceased; his life is a completed past period).
7. **have you known** (*know* is a stative verb in an ongoing relationship question).
8. **have already bought** (*already* indicates completion prior to now).
9. **lost** (*yesterday evening* specifies past time).
10. **has hired** (*so far this month* is an unfinished time period up to now).

### Section 2
11. **was cooking / rang** (Long background action interrupted by a sudden point action).
12. **was raining / stepped** (Background weather condition ongoing when exiting cinema).
13. **broke / was playing** (Sudden accident during continuous sports activity).
14. **were you doing** (Action in progress at a specific past point in time: *at eight o'clock*).
15. **were discussing** (Continuous activity ongoing when teacher entered).
16. **was walking / bumped** (Walking interrupted by meeting classmate).
17. **was writing** (Activity ongoing when the crash happened).
18. **were watching** (Activity in progress when the power went off).

### Section 3
19. **is going to rain** (Prediction based on immediate physical evidence: *black clouds*).
20. **will answer / 'll answer** (Spontaneous decision made at the moment of speaking).
21. **am flying** (Fixed schedule/arrangement involving bought tickets).
22. **are you doing / are you going to do** (Inquiry about established future plans).
23. **will not tell / won't tell** (Promise formula: *I promise I will/won't...*).
24. **are going to renovate** (Pre-planned intention; money already saved).
25. **will go / 'll go** (Decision made right now based on feeling tired).

### Section 4
26. **must / have to** (Legal obligation).
27. **don't have to** (No obligation; optional).
28. **mustn't** (Strict prohibition; dangerous).
29. **should** (Advice / recommendation).
30. **mustn't / cannot** (Prohibited by zoo regulations).
31. **don't have to** (Absence of uniform obligation).
32. **must / should** (Internal personal obligation to save).

### Section 5
33. **boils** (Zero conditional: scientific certainty).
34. **will pass** (First conditional: real future possibility).
35. **won** (Second conditional if-clause requires Past Simple).
36. **will cancel** (First conditional result clause).
37. **were** (Second conditional hypothetical subjunctive: *If I were you*).
38. **do not receive / don't receive** (Zero conditional natural fact).
39. **would learn** (Second conditional result clause).
40. **hurries** (*Unless* = *If not*; requires Present Simple).

### Section 6
41. **Delicious mangoes are grown in Kampong Speu (by farmers).** (Present Simple passive: *are + V3*).
42. **The central post office was designed by a famous French architect.** (Past Simple passive: *was + V3*).
43. **Angkor Wat is visited by millions of tourists every year.**
44. **The motorbike was not repaired by the mechanic yesterday.**
45. **Is English spoken in Singapore?**
46. **A new national highway was constructed in 2023.**

### Section 7
47. **This is the woman who (or that) manages the new bookstore.**
48. **Kampot is a charming town which (or that) is famous for its black pepper.**
49. **That is the hospital where my younger brother was born.**
50. **I met a talented musician whose band plays traditional Khmer music.**
51. **Here is the laptop (which / that) I bought on discount last Friday.**

### Section 8
52. **to postpone** (*decide + to-infinitive*).
53. **answering** (*avoid + gerund*).
54. **inviting** (Preposition *for* requires gerund `-ing`).
55. **meeting** (*look forward to* takes gerund; *to* is a preposition).
56. **to finish** (*manage + to-infinitive*).
57. **saying** (Preposition *without* requires gerund `-ing`).
58. **closing** (*mind + gerund*).
59. **to help** (*promise + to-infinitive*).

### Section 9
60. **is more expensive than** (Opposite comparative).
61. **is the highest** (Superlative transformation).
62. **as tall as** (Equality structure: *as + adj + as*).
63. **too heavy for** (*too + adj + for someone to do*).
64. **old enough** (*not + adj + enough to do*).

### Section 10
65. **I completely agree with your proposal.** (*Agree* is a verb, not an adjective).
66. **She didn't buy any vegetables at the market.** (After auxiliary *didn't*, use base verb *buy*).
67. **Every student in the classroom has a dictionary.** (*Every* takes a singular countable noun: *student*).
68. **Although it was raining, we enjoyed our trip.** (Never combine *Although* with *but*).
69. **Because he was sick, he couldn't take the test.** (Never combine *Because* with *so*).
70. **How long have you lived in this city?** (*How long* directly modifies the question without *time*).
