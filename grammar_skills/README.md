## 📊 Level-by-Level Grammar Framework

| Level | CEFR Target | Exam Equivalent | Core Grammatical Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B1** | Intermediate (មធ្យម) | Cambridge PET / IELTS 4.5–5.0 | Past vs Present Perfect, narrative continuous, 1st/2nd conditionals, simple passives, relative clauses |
| **B2** | Upper Intermediate (មធ្យមកម្រិតខ្ពស់) | Cambridge FCE / IELTS 5.5–6.5 | Aspectual duration vs result, past modal deduction, mixed conditionals, reporting passives, causatives, pied-piping |
| **C1** | Advanced (កម្រិតខ្ពស់) | Cambridge CAE / IELTS 7.0–8.0 | Negative inversion, It/Wh-cleft sentences, participle compression, mandative subjunctive, inverted conditionals |
| **C2** | Proficiency (កម្រិតជំនាញកំពូល) | Cambridge CPE / GRE Verbal 160+ | Locative inversion, nominative absolute constructions, comparative inversion, litotes, archaic subjunctives |

## 🚀 How to Study This Curriculum

1. **Phase 1 — Master the Technique Guide (`technique/`):** Study the structural blueprints, comparison charts, and common error corrections for your level.
2. **Phase 2 — Examine the Mock Exam Passage (`examples/`):** Read the authentic ~300-word contextual text to see how grammar functions naturally in discourse. Then complete the 4-part Use of English examination.
3. **Phase 3 — Complete Intensive Exercises (`exercises/`):** Work through the categorized exercises and sentence transformations, then study the detailed answer explanations.
