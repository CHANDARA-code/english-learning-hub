## 🎧 Best Practices for Mock Exam Simulation

1. **Simulate Test Conditions:**
   - Use over-ear headphones in a quiet room.
   - Play each audio track **once only** without pausing, rewinding, or slowing down.
2. **Preview Questions:**
   - Use the allocated 30–45 seconds at the start of each section to underline keywords and predict word classes (nouns, numbers, adjectives).
3. **Write as You Listen:**
   - Record answers directly on scrap paper or typed into a local document.
4. **10-Minute Transfer & Self-Check:**
   - Verify singular/plural endings (e.g. *consultations*, *corners*, *cheeses*), capital letters for proper nouns (e.g. *Bruno*, *Pine Park*, *May 11th*), and exact word limits (*NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS*).
5. **Analyze Errors with Audioscripts:**
   - Refer to the acoustic decoding trap analysis in each guide to identify whether your error was caused by connected speech (linking, elision), a distractor/self-correction, or an unfamiliar vocabulary item.
