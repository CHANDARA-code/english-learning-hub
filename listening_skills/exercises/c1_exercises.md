## Tier 2 — Advanced Broadcast Audio Tasks

### Audio Script: Forum on Linguistic Homogenization in the Digital Sphere

> **[00:00] PRESENTER (British RP):** Welcome to *Ideas and Society*. Today we examine how generative language models are subtly reshaping human linguistic diversity. Dr. Elena Rostova, you’ve argued that digital translation is eroding minority linguistic idioms.
>
> **[00:15] DR. ROSTOVA (Slavic-accented English):** Precisely. What technology evangelists hail as seamless intercultural bridge-building is, in truth, an engine of aggressive syntactic flattening. When algorithms ingest vast corpora dominated by Silicon Valley English, they map nuance through an overwhelmingly Anglocentric lens. Subtle conceptual distinctions embedded in indigenous languages are discarded as computational noise.
>
> **[00:39] DR. THORNE (General American):** Well, Elena, while I grant that algorithmic bias is pervasive, painting digital translation as purely imperialistic strikes me as an oversimplification. In post-conflict societies, machine translation allows grassroots NGOs to bypass authoritarian censorship and distribute human rights dossiers across twenty regional dialects simultaneously.
>
> **[01:02] DR. ROSTOVA:** That’s an instrumental defense, Marcus! It conflates operational utility with cultural survival. A language is not merely an encryption cipher for transmitting geopolitical propaganda; it is an irreplaceable ontological vessel. Once an algorithmic intermediary conditions a generation of speakers to think in translated English idioms, the indigenous epistemic architecture is extinguished forever.

## ✅ Answer Key with Auditory Analysis

### Tier 1 Solutions
1. **not quite** (Glottal stop /ʔ/ replacing /t/ in both words).
2. **critical data** (Alveolar flapping of /t/ to voiced tap /ɾ/).
3. **the government stance** (Glottal stop on *government*).
4. **water bottle** (Double flapping: *water* and *bottle*).
5. **what you mean** (Coalescence: /t/ + /j/ $\rightarrow$ /tʃ/).
6. **Answer: B** (*"not entirely unproblematic"* is classic litotes meaning catastrophically flawed).
7. **Answer: B** (*"stroke of absolute genius"* with cynical tone signals biting sarcasm).
8. **Answer: A** (*"destined to gather dust"* implies a futile waste of money).
9. **The airline definitely knew that its software was defective.** (Rhetorical question indicating undeniable guilt).
10. **Nobody in their right mind would invest in an unpatented battery design.** (Negative rhetorical assertion).
11. **Clause B** (The core evaluation follows *but*).
12. **Critical** (The concession *While luminous* is dismissed; the true verdict is *remarkably hollow and derivative*).

### Tier 2 Solutions
13. **Answer: B** ([00:15]: *"an engine of aggressive syntactic flattening... Anglocentric lens"*).
14. **Answer: B** ([00:39]: *"painting digital translation as purely imperialistic strikes me as an oversimplification"*).
15. **Answer: B** ([01:02]: *"not merely an encryption cipher... an irreplaceable ontological vessel"* — ontology refers to the nature of being/existence).
16. **Anglocentric** ([00:15]: *"through an overwhelmingly Anglocentric lens"*).
17. **computational noise** ([00:15]: *"discarded as computational noise"*).
18. **authoritarian censorship** ([00:39]: *"bypass authoritarian censorship"*).
19. **indigenous epistemic** ([01:02]: *"indigenous epistemic architecture is extinguished"*).
20. **B (Both)** (Rostova argues it directly; Thorne concedes *"I grant that algorithmic bias is pervasive"*).
21. **T (Dr. Thorne)** (Introduced the human rights dossiers example).
22. **R (Dr. Rostova)** (Argues cultural extinction outweighs instrumental utility).
