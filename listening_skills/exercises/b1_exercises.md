## Tier 1 — Bottom-Up Acoustic & Phonetic Drills

### Exercise 1: Numbers & Times Acoustic Trap (-teen vs -ty)
*Read the phonetic transcriptions of spoken dialogue extracts. Determine whether the speaker said the **-teen** (13–19) or **-ty** (30–90) number.*

1. *"We need to purchase /fɔːˈtiːn/ new textbooks for the class."*  
   👉 Number: **14** or **40**? __________
2. *"The museum admission fee for adults is /ˈfɪfti/ dollars."*  
   👉 Number: **15** or **50**? __________
3. *"The flight from Bangkok will arrive at /sɪks-θɜːˈtiːn/ PM."*  
   👉 Time: **6:13** or **6:30**? __________
4. *"My grandmother was born in nineteen /ˈseventi/."*  
   👉 Year: **1917** or **1970**? __________
5. *"There are /ˈeɪti/ competitors registered for the race."*  
   👉 Number: **18** or **80**? __________

## Tier 2 — Top-Down Authentic Audio Scripts & Exam Tasks

### Audio Script A: Riverside Youth Hostel Enquiries

> **[00:00] RECEPTIONIST:** Riverside Youth Hostel, good afternoon. How can I help?  
> **[00:05] CALLER:** Hello! I’d like to reserve a bed in your dormitory for three nights starting next Tuesday, please.  
> **[00:12] RECEPTIONIST:** Certainly. We have space in our mixed dorm or our female-only dorm.  
> **[00:17] CALLER:** Mixed dorm is fine. How much is that per night?  
> **[00:21] RECEPTIONIST:** It was nineteen pounds last season, but our current standard rate is twenty-two pounds, which includes hot showers and high-speed Wi-Fi. Breakfast is an additional three pounds fifty if you want it.  
> **[00:35] CALLER:** I’ll definitely take the breakfast. My name is Sophie Thornton. That’s T-H-O-R-N-T-O-N.  
> **[00:46] RECEPTIONIST:** Great, Sophie. And what time do you expect to arrive?  
> **[00:50] CALLER:** My train gets into Central Station at five-fifteen, so I should be at the hostel by six o'clock in the evening.  
> **[00:58] RECEPTIONIST:** Excellent. Just note that our front desk closes for check-ins at ten PM.

### Audio Script B: Public Transport Radio Announcement

> **[00:00] ANNOUNCER:** And now for an urgent update for morning commuters traveling on the northern metro line. Due to essential signaling upgrades at Victoria Station, all trains on the Blue Line between Oakridge and Highfield will be suspended until two o'clock this afternoon. Replacement shuttle buses are operating every ten minutes from bus stop C outside Oakridge station. Passengers are strongly advised to allow an extra thirty minutes for their journey. ## ✅ Answer Key with Auditory Explanations

### Tier 1 Solutions
1. **14** (Stress on second syllable: /fɔːˈtiːn/).
2. **50** (Stress on first syllable: /ˈfɪfti/).
3. **6:13** (/sɪks-θɜːˈtiːn/ indicates thirteen).
4. **1970** (/ˈseventi/ indicates seventy).
5. **80** (/ˈeɪti/ indicates eighty).
6. **can** (/kən/ is the weak form of *can*).
7. **of** (/əv/ is the weak form of *of*).
8. **to** (/tə/ is the weak form of *to*).
9. **for** (/fə/ is the weak form of *for*).
10. **was** (/wəz/ is the weak form of *was*).
11. **put it on** (Consonant-to-vowel catenation).
12. **first of all**
13. **turn it off**
14. **wait a minute**
15. **Friday** (Self-correction: *"oh, hang on... let's make it Friday"*).
16. **18 / eighteen** (Self-correction: £25 is weekend; £18 is weekday).
17. **54** (Self-correction: 42 is sister's address; 54 is speaker's).
18. **BRADSHAW**
19. **CB2 1TN**
20. **kate.hughes@web.com**

### Tier 2 Solutions
21. **3 / three** ([00:05]: *"reserve a bed... for three nights"*).
22. **22 / twenty-two** ([00:21]: *"standard rate is twenty-two pounds"* — £19 was last season's rate).
23. **breakfast** ([00:35]: *"I’ll definitely take the breakfast"*).
24. **6:00 / six / 6** ([00:50]: *"should be at the hostel by six o'clock"* — 5:15 is the train arrival time).
25. **Answer: B** ([00:00]: *"essential signaling upgrades at Victoria Station"*).
26. **Answer: A** ([00:00]: *"shuttle buses are operating every ten minutes"* — 30 minutes is the extra journey time).
27. **TRUE** ([00:00]: *"suspended until two o'clock this afternoon"*).
28. **FALSE** ([00:00]: *"can also use their tickets on municipal buses without paying any surcharge"*).
29. **DOESN'T SAY** (The announcer does not mention whether upgrades are ahead of schedule).
30. **TRUE** ([00:00]: *"from bus stop C outside Oakridge station"*).
