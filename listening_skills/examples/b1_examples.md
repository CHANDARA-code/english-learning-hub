## Spoken Audio Transcript with Phonetic & Connected Speech Annotations

**Track Context:** An enquiry at an eco-tourism adventure desk in Siem Reap regarding a cycling tour.  
**Transcript Word Count:** 308 words  
**Audio Speed:** ~135 words per minute  

> **[00:00] AGENT:** Good morning! Welcome to Tonle Eco-Adventures. How can I help you today?  
>
> **[00:06] CUSTOMER:** Hello! I’m interested in booking your countryside cycling tour for tomorrow. I saw a brochure at my hotel, but I had a few questions about the departure time and pricing.  
>
> **[00:18] AGENT:** Of course! We run two daily cycling excursions. Our morning tour used to depart at seven o'clock, but because of recent road maintenance, we’ve shifted it to half past eight *(linking: /hɑːf-pɑːst-eɪt/)*. The afternoon tour leaves at two-fifteen.  
>
> **[00:35] CUSTOMER:** Half past eight in the morning sounds perfect. How long does the ride take?  
>
> **[00:41] AGENT:** The full circuit is approximately four hours, covering eighteen *(phonetic stress: /eɪˈtiːn/ - NOT eighty)* kilometers at an easy, relaxing pace. We stop at a traditional lotus farm and an ancient village pagoda where you can sample fresh tropical fruit.  
>
> **[00:58] CUSTOMER:** That sounds lovely. What is the total cost per participant?  
>
> **[01:03] AGENT:** Normally it is thirty-five dollars, but because you are staying at a partner hotel, you qualify for our discounted rate of twenty-eight dollars *(weak form: /əv ˈtwenti-eɪt ˈdɒləz/)*. That includes a mountain helmet, bottled water, and a licensed local guide.  
>
> **[01:21] CUSTOMER:** Excellent. I’d like to book for two people, please. My name is Arthur Pendelton. That’s P-E-N-D-E-L-T-O-N.  
>
> **[01:34] AGENT:** Wonderful, Mr. Pendelton. And could I have a contact telephone number?  
>
> **[01:39] CUSTOMER:** Certainly. It’s 0-7-7, 4-2-9, 8-1-5 *(spoken: /əʊ-ˈsevn-ˈsevn/)*. Oh wait, apologies, that’s my old office landline! My current mobile is 0-7-7, 4-2-9, 8-5-1.  
>
> **[01:57] AGENT:** 0-7-7, 4-2-9, 8-5-1. Perfect. Please arrive at our office fifteen minutes before departure tomorrow to fit your bicycles.

## Answer Key, Auditory Evidence & Distractor Deconstruction

### Part 1: Form Completion
1. **8:30 / half past eight**  
   - **Auditory Evidence ([00:18]):** *"Our morning tour used to depart at seven o'clock... we’ve shifted it to half past eight."*  
   - **Distractor:** 7:00 was the *past* departure time.
2. **18 / eighteen**  
   - **Auditory Evidence ([00:41]):** *"...covering eighteen kilometers at an easy, relaxing pace."*  
   - **Phonetic Note:** Candidate must not write 80.
3. **28 / twenty-eight**  
   - **Auditory Evidence ([01:03]):** *"Normally it is thirty-five dollars, but... discounted rate of twenty-eight dollars."*  
   - **Distractor:** $35 is the regular, non-discounted rate.
4. **077429851 / 077 429 851**  
   - **Auditory Evidence ([01:39]):** *"It’s 0-7-7, 4-2-9, 8-1-5. Oh wait, apologies... My current mobile is 0-7-7, 4-2-9, 8-5-1."*  
   - **Distractor:** 077 429 815 was the customer's old office landline.

---

### Part 2: Multiple Choice
5. **Answer: B**  
   - **Evidence ([00:18]):** *"...because of recent road maintenance, we’ve shifted it to half past eight."*  
   - **Paraphrase:** *"road maintenance"* = *repair work on local roads*.
6. **Answer: A**  
   - **Evidence ([00:41]):** *"...pagoda where you can sample fresh tropical fruit."*
7. **Answer: C**  
   - **Evidence ([01:03]):** *"...because you are staying at a partner hotel, you qualify for our discounted rate..."*  
   - **Paraphrase:** *"partner hotel"* = *affiliated hotel*.
8. **Answer: B**  
   - **Evidence ([01:57]):** *"Please arrive at our office fifteen minutes before departure tomorrow to fit your bicycles."*
