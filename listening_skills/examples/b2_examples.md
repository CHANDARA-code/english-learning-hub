## Spoken Audio Transcript with Acoustic & Intonation Annotations

**Track Context:** University tutorial between an environmental science student (Marcus) and his academic supervisor (Dr. Vance) evaluating an urban microplastics research proposal.  
**Transcript Word Count:** 312 words  
**Audio Speed:** ~165 words per minute  

> **[00:00] DR. VANCE (Australian):** Come in, Marcus. Take a seat. I’ve reviewed your draft proposal on tracking microplastic residue in urban waterways. Overall, the theoretical framework is sound, but I have serious reservations about your proposed sampling methodology *(falling-rising intonation: /ˌmeθəˈdɒlədʒi/ ↘↗)*.
>
> **[00:16] MARCUS (American):** Thanks, Dr. Vance. I was actually worried about that. Originally, I planned to collect samples *(elision: /kəˈlek ˈsæmplz/ - /t/ dropped)* exclusively from storm drains near industrial factories. 
>
> **[00:26] DR. VANCE:** Yes, and while that seems logical on paper, it produces heavily skewed data. Industrial zones are cleaned periodically, whereas residential runoff carries an immense volume of synthetic fibers from domestic laundry. You’d get a far more representative sample by monitoring suburban culverts.
>
> **[00:46] MARCUS:** That makes total sense. I suppose my biggest constraint right now is access to the spectroscopy lab *(assimilation: /læb/ instead of /læbərətɔːri/)*. The department head told me the optical microscope was booked through next month *(elision: /neks mʌnθ/)*.
>
> **[00:58] DR. VANCE:** Don’t panic. We’ve just acquired an automated infrared spectrometer through an Australian research grant *(intrusive /r/: /ɔːˈstreɪliən-r-ɪnˈfrɑːred/)*. It’s housed in the basement annex, and graduate students can book four-hour slots starting next Monday. 
>
> **[01:14] MARCUS:** That would save me dozens of hours of manual counting! What about the statistical analysis? Should I use standard regression models?
>
> **[01:23] DR. VANCE:** Actually, because environmental distribution is non-linear, standard regression will lead you astray. I’d strongly recommend employing cluster analysis *(contrastive stress on CLUSTER)*. Have a look at Dr. Liam Thorne’s recent paper on marine sediments; his mathematical modeling is exemplary.
>
> **[01:42] MARCUS:** I’ll download that from the library repository this afternoon. Thanks so much for pointing me in the right direction!

## Acoustic Dissection: Elision, Assimilation & Intrusive Linking

| Auditory Feature | Spoken Occurrence in Script | Realized Phonetic Output | Listening Comprehension Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alveolar Elision (/t/ dropped)** | [00:16] *"collect samples"* | /kəˈlek ˈsæmplz/ | The /t/ sound completely disappears before /s/. Intermediate listeners struggle to recognize the root verb *collect*. |
| **Consonant Cluster Elision** | [00:46] *"next month"* | /neks mʌnθ/ | The /t/ in *next* is dropped, blending directly into the /m/ of *month*. |
| **Intrusive /r/ Linking** | [00:58] *"Australia and..."* / *"Australian infrared"*| /ɔːˈstreɪliən-r-ɪnˈfrɑːred/ | An unwritten /r/ bridges the final vowel of *Australian* to the initial vowel of *infrared*. |
| **Contrastive Tonic Stress** | [01:23] *"CLUSTER analysis"* | /ˈklʌstər əˈnæləsɪs/ | Sudden high pitch on *CLUSTER* to distinguish it from the student's suggestion (*regression*). |

## Full B2 Listening Mock Examination (8 Items)

### Part 1: Sentence Completion (Questions 1–4)
*Complete the sentences below. Write **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS** from the recording for each answer.*

1. Dr. Vance expresses concern regarding Marcus's proposed ________________________ for gathering water specimens.  
2. Marcus is advised to collect samples from ________________________ to ensure more representative data.  
3. The newly acquired infrared spectrometer is located in the ________________________.  
4. Dr. Vance recommends that Marcus utilize ________________________ rather than standard regression models.

### Part 2: Multiple Choice with Subtle Distractors (Questions 5–8)
*Choose the correct letter, **A, B, or C**.*

**5. Why does Dr. Vance advise against sampling solely from industrial storm drains?**  
A. Industrial factories strictly prohibit student researchers.  
B. Frequent municipal cleaning distorts the true volume of waste.  
C. Synthetic fibers are impossible to detect in chemical waste.  

**6. What initial obstacle did Marcus anticipate regarding laboratory access?**  
A. The optical microscope was fully reserved by other scholars.  
B. The department demanded an excessive laboratory usage fee.  
C. The spectroscopy equipment had suffered mechanical failure.  

**7. How will the newly acquired equipment assist Marcus in his research?**  
A. It will eliminate the need to collect physical samples.  
B. It will dramatically reduce the time spent counting particles manually.  
C. It allows remote access from home through an Australian grant.  

**8. Why does Dr. Vance mention Dr. Liam Thorne's published study?**  
A. To criticize its experimental mathematical calculations.  
B. To warn Marcus that his topic has already been investigated.  
C. To provide a superior model of non-linear statistical analysis.

   - **Evidence ([00:58]):** *"It’s housed in the basement annex, and graduate students can book four-hour slots..."*

   - **Evidence ([01:23]):** *"I’d strongly recommend employing cluster analysis."*

### Part 2: Multiple Choice
5. **Answer: B**  
   - **Evidence ([00:26]):** *"Industrial zones are cleaned periodically, whereas residential runoff carries an immense volume of synthetic fibers..."*  
   - **Distractor Analysis:** A is never mentioned; C contradicts the reality that synthetic fibers come from residential laundry.
6. **Answer: A**  
   - **Evidence ([00:46]):** *"The department head told me the optical microscope was booked through next month."*
7. **Answer: B**  
   - **Evidence ([01:14]):** *"That would save me dozens of hours of manual counting!"*
8. **Answer: C**  
   - **Evidence ([01:23]):** *"...Have a look at Dr. Liam Thorne’s recent paper on marine sediments; his mathematical modeling is exemplary."* (*Exemplary* = serving as a desirable model).
