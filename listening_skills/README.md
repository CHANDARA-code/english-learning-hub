# 🎧 Listening Skills Mastery Program — B1 to C2
## មគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍អភិវឌ្ឍន៍ជំនាញស្តាប់ភាសាអង់គ្លេស (កម្រិត B1 ដល់ C2)

Welcome to the **Listening Skills Mastery Program**, structured comprehensively according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (**CEFR**). This resource equips learners with core acoustic decoding strategies, authentic downloadable IELTS Listening audio exam packs, ~300-word dual-speaker spoken dialogue MP3s with full exam tasks, and a two-tier practice system combining bottom-up phonetics and top-down academic comprehension.

## 🚀 How to Study This Curriculum

1. **Phase 1 — Master the Technique Guide (`technique/`):** Learn the phonetic realities of connected speech, intonation shifts, and exam distractor deconstruction for your target level.
2. **Phase 2 — Study the Mock Exam Transcript & Play Audio (`examples/` & `audio/examples/`):** Listen to the dual-speaker MP3 while reading the transcript with timestamps and phonetic annotations.
3. **Phase 3 — Take the Full IELTS Listening Mock Exam (`audio/ielts_test_01/`):** Put on headphones, play Sections 1 through 4, complete all 40 questions in [ielts_test_01_guide.md](file:///Users/chandara-dgc/Documents/learn-english/listening_skills/audio/ielts_test_01/ielts_test_01_guide.md), and check your IELTS band score.
4. **Phase 4 — Complete Two-Tier Exercises (`exercises/`):** Drill bottom-up phonetics (Tier 1) to train your ear on real-time sound mutations, then tackle full academic audio tasks (Tier 2).
