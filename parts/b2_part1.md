## 🔴 Level B2 — Upper Intermediate (Words 3501–5000)

### B2 Part 1: Academic Language (3501–3750)

### 3501. analyze /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** វិភាគ
1. If researchers analyze the statistical data thoroughly, they will identify patterns that were previously overlooked.
2. The committee cannot reach a sound decision until all submitted laboratory samples are analyzed independently.
3. Although the software is designed to analyze complex demographic trends, human oversight is still required to interpret subtle anomalies.

---

### 3502. synthesize /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** សំយោគ
1. In order to complete the dissertation, students must synthesize findings from dozens of peer-reviewed articles into a unified framework.
2. If you do not synthesize your sources effectively, your literature review will read like a disjointed list of summaries.
3. The conference aims to synthesize various theoretical perspectives that have emerged across different social science disciplines.

---

### 3503. evaluate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** វាយតម្លៃ
1. Before any curriculum changes are implemented, educators must carefully evaluate their potential impact on student performance.
2. If independent auditors evaluate the project proposals, funding will be allocated much more objectively.
3. The study was designed to evaluate whether cognitive behavioral interventions could produce lasting psychological benefits.

---

### 3504. assess /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** វាយតម្លៃ / កត់សម្គាល់កម្រិត
1. Unless the board assesses the environmental risks beforehand, the construction project may face significant regulatory penalties.
2. Psychologists use standardized testing instruments to assess verbal reasoning skills in bilingual children.
3. If we assess the long-term feasibility of the proposal, we can determine whether additional resources will be needed.

---

### 3505. justify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បញ្ជាក់ហេតុផល / ផ្ដល់សំអាង
1. The author failed to provide adequate empirical evidence to justify the sweeping conclusions presented in the final chapter.
2. If an investigator proposes an intrusive experimental method, they must justify why less disruptive alternatives were rejected.
3. Scholars are expected to justify their methodological choices when defending their dissertations before the academic committee.

---

### 3506. argue /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** លើកហេតុផលតវ៉ា
1. Several prominent economists argue that inflation cannot be controlled without tightening fiscal policy across the eurozone.
2. If the defense attorney argues that the evidence was mishandled, the judge might dismiss the entire case.
3. Although many commentators argue that technology reduces social interaction, recent sociological studies suggest a more nuanced picture.

---

### 3507. contend /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ប្រកែក / អះអាង
1. Proponents of the new theory contend that quantum effects play an essential role in biological processes.
2. If rival researchers contend that the initial calculations were flawed, a public academic debate will likely ensue.
3. The authors contend that institutional inequality cannot be dismantled unless systemic reforms are simultaneously enacted.

---

### 3508. assert /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បញ្ជាក់អះអាង
1. The lead scientist asserted that the new compound exhibited significant antimicrobial activity without causing cytotoxicity.
2. If you assert a controversial thesis in academic writing, you must substantiate it with verifiable empirical data.
3. Critics asserted that the policy had been implemented prematurely before adequate pilot testing was conducted.

---

### 3509. claim /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** អះអាង
1. Although the pharmaceutical manufacturer claims that the medication has no side effects, clinical trials suggest otherwise.
2. If an author claims that a correlation implies causation, the peer reviewers will almost certainly reject the manuscript.
3. Scholars who claim that ancient civilizations possessed advanced metallurgy must produce concrete archaeological evidence.

---

### 3510. hypothesize /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ដាក់សម្មតិកម្ម
1. The research team hypothesized that exposure to natural daylight would enhance cognitive focus among office workers.
2. If we hypothesize that genetic factors outweigh environmental influences, our experimental model must isolate those variables.
3. Neuroscientists hypothesized that memory consolidation occurs predominantly during specific stages of deep sleep.

---

### 3511. theorize /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បង្កើតទ្រឹស្តី
1. Astrophysicists theorize that dark matter comprises a substantial portion of the total mass in the observable universe.
2. If sociologists theorize about modern urbanization, they must take digital connectivity and remote work into account.
3. Early philosophers theorized that matter consisted of indivisible particles long before atomic structure was experimentally confirmed.

---

### 3512. postulate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** សន្មតជាគោលការណ៍
1. The economic model postulates that consumers will always behave rationally when maximizing personal utility.
2. If researchers postulate that cultural values dictate consumer spending, they must test this assumption across diverse societies.
3. Linguists postulate that all human languages share an underlying structural blueprint known as universal grammar.

---

### 3513. demonstrate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បង្ហាញ / បញ្ជាក់បង្ហាញ
1. The laboratory experiments clearly demonstrated that the catalyst significantly accelerated the chemical reaction rate.
2. If future trials demonstrate the efficacy of the vaccine, health authorities will grant emergency authorization.
3. By presenting comprehensive comparative graphs, the speaker demonstrated how renewable energy costs have plummeted over the last decade.

---

### 3514. illustrate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ពន្យល់ដោយឧទាហរណ៍ / បង្ហាញ
1. The professor used historical case studies to illustrate how economic embargoes often produce unintended humanitarian consequences.
2. If diagrams are included to illustrate the biochemical pathway, undergraduate students will grasp the concept much faster.
3. The report illustrates the stark disparities in healthcare access that persist between rural and urban communities.

---

### 3515. exemplify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** លើកជាគំរូ
1. The rapid development of mRNA vaccines exemplifies how international scientific collaboration can yield breakthrough innovations.
2. If an institution aims to exemplify excellence in teaching, it must provide continuous pedagogical support to its faculty.
3. The novelist's intricate narrative structure exemplifies the core aesthetic tenets of modernist literature.

---

### 3516. elaborate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** លម្អិតបន្ថែម
1. The presenter was requested to elaborate on the statistical methodology that had been applied during the third phase of the trial.
2. If you do not elaborate on your theoretical framework, readers may struggle to understand your analytical perspective.
3. In the subsequent chapter, the author elaborates on how macroeconomic instability contributed to the political crisis.

---

### 3517. clarify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បំភ្លឺឱ្យច្បាស់
1. The editor requested that the researchers clarify their operational definitions before the article could be accepted for publication.
2. If the instructions do not clarify the grading rubric, students might submit assignments that fail to meet academic standards.
3. The government spokesperson issued a detailed statement to clarify how the new taxation laws would affect university endowments.

---

### 3518. specify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** បញ្ជាក់ជាក់លាក់
1. Academic guidelines specify that all direct quotations must include the exact page number from the original source.
2. If the contract does not specify the timeline for project deliverables, disputes may arise between the collaborating institutions.
3. The research protocol clearly specifies which exclusion criteria should be applied when screening potential volunteers.

---

### 3519. define /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** កំណត់និយមន័យ
1. Before launching an empirical investigation, researchers must clearly define the operational variables they intend to measure.
2. If sociologists define poverty solely through income metrics, they will fail to capture multidimensional aspects of deprivation.
3. The introductory chapter defines several technical terms that appear frequently throughout the specialized discourse.

---

### 3520. categorize /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ចាត់ជាជំពូក / បែងចែកក្រុម
1. The archivists categorized historical manuscripts according to their geographical origin, language, and estimated date of creation.
2. If we categorize respondents into distinct age cohorts, we can observe generational shifts in political attitudes.
3. Botanists use morphological features to categorize newly discovered plant specimens into appropriate taxonomic families.

---

### 3521. classify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ចាត់ថ្នាក់
1. Medical pathologists classify tumors based on their histological characteristics and biological behavior.
2. If machine learning algorithms classify customer feedback accurately, support teams can resolve critical issues more efficiently.
3. The library system classifies non-fiction volumes according to the Dewey Decimal Classification system.

---

### 3522. compare /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ប្រៀបធៀប
1. The objective of the paper was to compare educational outcomes between public institutions and private charter schools.
2. If analysts compare the current economic indicators with those of 2008, significant structural differences become apparent.
3. Scholars frequently compare democratic transitions across Eastern Europe to determine common institutional catalysts.

---

### 3523. contrast /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ប្រៀបធៀបភាពផ្ទុយគ្នា
1. The author contrasts centralized administrative systems with decentralized governance models to demonstrate their relative efficiency.
2. If you contrast qualitative interviews with quantitative surveys, you will recognize the unique strengths and limitations of each method.
3. The essay contrasts the optimistic outlook of Enlightenment thinkers with the skepticism of twentieth-century existentialists.

---

### 3524. distinguish /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** ញែកឱ្យដាច់ / បែងចែក
1. It is essential to distinguish between empirical correlation and direct causation when interpreting statistical models.
2. If clinicians cannot distinguish viral infections from bacterial pathogens, they may prescribe unnecessary antibiotic treatments.
3. Philosophers often distinguish normative ethical theories from descriptive accounts of moral behavior.

---

### 3525. differentiate /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** កំណត់ភាពខុសគ្នា
1. Advanced diagnostic tools allow physicians to differentiate between benign lesions and malignant growths with high precision.
2. If consumer brands fail to differentiate their products in crowded markets, their profit margins will steadily decline.
3. The study attempted to differentiate the psychological effects of acute stress from those caused by chronic exhaustion.

---

### 3526. identify /verb/
**ន័យខ្មែរ:** កំណត់អត្តសញ្ញាណ / រកឃើញ
1. Through genetic sequencing, molecular biologists identified several mutations responsible for inherited cardiac conditions.

### 3530. consider /verb/
