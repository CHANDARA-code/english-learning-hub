# Set 1: 25 Common Daily English Expressions & Idioms
*(ឃ្លា និងពាក្យស្លោកភាសាអង់គ្លេសទូទៅ ២៥ ប្រើប្រាស់ក្នុងការសន្ទនាប្រចាំថ្ងៃ)*

---

## Vocabulary & Expression Guide

### 1. Break the ice
- **English Definition**: To make people feel more comfortable and relaxed in a social setting or meeting.
- **Khmer Translation**: ធ្វើឱ្យបរិយាកាសស្និទ្ធស្នាល ឬបំបាត់ភាពទើសទាល់
- **Example**: *At the beginning of the workshop, the teacher played a quick game to **break the ice**.*

---

### 2. Bite the bullet
- **English Definition**: To force yourself to do something unpleasant or difficult that cannot be avoided.
- **Khmer Translation**: បង្ខំចិត្តទ្រាំ ឬសម្រេចចិត្តប្រឈមមុខនឹងស្ថានភាពលំបាកដែលជៀសមិនរួច
- **Example**: *I really hate going to the dentist, but I just have to **bite the bullet** and go.*

---

### 3. Call it a day
- **English Definition**: To stop working on something for the rest of the day.
- **Khmer Translation**: សម្រាក ឬបញ្ចប់ការងារសម្រាប់ថ្ងៃនេះ
- **Example**: *We’ve been working on this report for six hours; let’s **call it a day**.*

---

### 4. Under the weather
- **English Definition**: Feeling slightly ill, sick, or unwell.
- **Khmer Translation**: មិនស្រួលខ្លួន ឬឈឺស្កាត់ស្រាល
- **Example**: *Sophea didn't come to class today because she was feeling a bit **under the weather**.*

---

### 5. Hit the nail on the head
- **English Definition**: To describe exactly what is causing a situation or to be completely accurate.
- **Khmer Translation**: និយាយចំចំណុចសំខាន់ ឬត្រូវបេះបិទ
- **Example**: *When David said our marketing strategy was outdated, he **hit the nail on the head**.*

---

### 6. Piece of cake
- **English Definition**: Something very easy and effortless to accomplish.
- **Khmer Translation**: ស្រួលដូចបកចេក / ងាយស្រួលបំផុត
- **Example**: *Don't worry about the driving test; it's a **piece of cake**.*

---

### 7. Cost an arm and a leg
- **English Definition**: To be extremely expensive.
- **Khmer Translation**: ថ្លៃកប់ពពក / ថ្លៃខ្លាំងណាស់
- **Example**: *Buying the latest flagship smartphone can **cost an arm and a leg**.*

---

### 8. Once in a blue moon
- **English Definition**: Very rarely or almost never.
- **Khmer Translation**: កម្រណាស់ / យូរៗម្តង
- **Example**: *My brother lives abroad, so I only get to see him **once in a blue moon**.*

---

### 9. Let the cat out of the bag
- **English Definition**: To accidentally or mistakenly reveal a secret.
- **Khmer Translation**: បែកធ្លាយការសម្ងាត់ដោយអចេតនា
- **Example**: *We were planning a surprise birthday party, but Dara accidentally **let the cat out of the bag**.*

---

### 10. See eye to eye
- **English Definition**: To agree fully with someone on an issue.
- **Khmer Translation**: យល់ស្របគ្នាទាំងស្រុង / មានទស្សនៈដូចគ្នា
- **Example**: *My manager and I don't always **see eye to eye**, but we respect each other's opinions.*

---

### 11. Spill the beans
- **English Definition**: To disclose confidential information or a secret.
- **Khmer Translation**: ទម្លាយរឿងសម្ងាត់
- **Example**: *Trust me with your secret—I promise I won't **spill the beans**.*

---

### 12. Burn the midnight oil
- **English Definition**: To work or study late into the night.
- **Khmer Translation**: ខំធ្វើការ ឬរៀនសូត្រដល់យប់ជ្រៅ
- **Example**: *The final exams start tomorrow, so many students are **burning the midnight oil**.*

---

### 13. Cut corners
- **English Definition**: To do something in the easiest, cheapest, or fastest way, often compromising quality or safety.
- **Khmer Translation**: ធ្វើកាត់ៗ ឬកាត់បន្ថយគុណភាពដើម្បីចំណេញពេល/លុយ
- **Example**: *The construction company **cut corners** on materials, and now the building has cracks.*

---

### 14. Back to the drawing board
- **English Definition**: To start over from the beginning because a previous plan or attempt failed.
- **Khmer Translation**: ចាប់ផ្តើមរៀបចំផែនការឡើងវិញពីដំបូង
- **Example**: *Our proposal was rejected by the client, so it’s **back to the drawing board**.*

---

### 15. A blessing in disguise
- **English Definition**: A misfortune or bad situation that eventually results in an unexpected benefit.
- **Khmer Translation**: រឿងអាក្រក់ដែលនាំឱ្យមានផលល្អទៅវិញ (គ្រោះក្លាយជាលាភ)
- **Example**: *Losing that job was **a blessing in disguise** because it led me to start my own successful business.*

---

### 16. Cross that bridge when you come to it
- **English Definition**: To deal with a problem only when it happens, rather than worrying about it beforehand.
- **Khmer Translation**: ទុកឱ្យដល់ពេលចាំដោះស្រាយ (កុំទាន់បារម្ភមុន)
- **Example**: *We might run out of budget next month, but let’s **cross that bridge when we come to it**.*

---

### 17. The best of both worlds
- **English Definition**: A situation where someone enjoys two different advantages at the same time.
- **Khmer Translation**: ទទួលបានផលប្រយោជន៍ល្អទាំងសងខាង
- **Example**: *Working remotely allows her to live near the beach while earning a city salary—she has **the best of both worlds**.*

---

### 18. Feel like a fish out of water
- **English Definition**: To feel uncomfortable, awkward, or out of place in an unfamiliar environment.
- **Khmer Translation**: មានអារម្មណ៍មិនស៊ាំ ឬទើសទាល់ក្នុងចំណោមអ្នកដទៃ
- **Example**: *As an engineer attending an art conference, I **felt like a fish out of water**.*

---

### 19. Barking up the wrong tree
- **English Definition**: Following the wrong path of inquiry or directing efforts toward the wrong person/solution.
- **Khmer Translation**: សង្ស័យខុស ឬស្វែងរកដំណោះស្រាយខុសកន្លែង
- **Example**: *If you think I took your notebook, you are **barking up the wrong tree**; I wasn't even in the room.*

---

### 20. Actions speak louder than words
- **English Definition**: What someone actually does is more meaningful and reliable than what they promise.
- **Khmer Translation**: ទង្វើសំខាន់ជាងពាក្យសម្តី (ធ្វើជាក់ស្តែងប្រសើរជាងនិយាយតែមាត់)
- **Example**: *He keeps promising to help, but **actions speak louder than words**.*

---

### 21. Kill two birds with one stone
- **English Definition**: To accomplish two different goals with a single action.
- **Khmer Translation**: បាញ់សត្វមួយគ្រាប់បានពីរ / ធ្វើរឿងមួយចំណេញការងារពីរ
- **Example**: *I can drop you off at work on my way to the supermarket and **kill two birds with one stone**.*

---

### 22. Through thick and thin
- **English Definition**: Through both good times and bad times; under all circumstances.
- **Khmer Translation**: រួមសុខរួមទុក្ខ / ទោះស្ថិតក្នុងកាលៈទេសៈលំបាកយ៉ាងណា
- **Example**: *They have been best friends for 20 years and have stood by each other **through thick and thin**.*

---

### 23. Speak of the devil
- **English Definition**: Said when a person appears right while they are being talked about.
- **Khmer Translation**: និយាយដើមគេដល់ / និយាយដល់ភ្លាម មកដល់ភ្លែត
- **Example**: *Did you hear about what Socheat did? Oh, **speak of the devil**, here he comes!*

---

### 24. Take it with a grain of salt
- **English Definition**: To view something with skepticism; not believe it completely.
- **Khmer Translation**: កុំជឿទាំងស្រុង / ស្តាប់ទុកពាក់កណ្តាល
- **Example**: *Online rumors can be misleading, so always **take them with a grain of salt**.*

---

### 25. Burn bridges
- **English Definition**: To permanently destroy relationships or paths to return, making future reconciliation impossible.
- **Khmer Translation**: ផ្តាច់ទំនាក់ទំនងទាំងស្រុងដែលមិនអាចវិលត្រឡប់ក្រោយ
- **Example**: *Even if you resign from your job in frustration, never **burn bridges** with your former colleagues.*
