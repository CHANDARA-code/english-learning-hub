## Part A — Sentence Skills

### Exercise 1: Choose the Linking Word

Choose the best word: **because · so · but · although · for example · however · also · then**

1. I was very tired, ________ I went to bed early.
2. She didn't come to the party ________ she was sick.
3. ________ it was raining, we played football.
4. Many fruits are good for you, ________ bananas and oranges.
5. The hotel was cheap. ________, the rooms were very small.
6. I like tea, ________ I don't like coffee.
7. First, boil the water. ________, add the noodles.
8. Cycling is good exercise. It is ________ cheap.

---

### Exercise 2: Join the Sentences

Join each pair into **one** sentence using the word in brackets.

1. I stayed at home. It was very cold. *(because)*
2. My brother is a doctor. He works in Phnom Penh. *(who)*
3. The film was long. I enjoyed it. *(although)*
4. You study every day. You will pass the exam. *(if)*
5. I finished my homework. I watched TV. *(after)*
6. This is the café. We first met here. *(where)*

## Part B — Paragraph Skills

### Exercise 4: Choose the Best Topic Sentence

Choose the best topic sentence (a, b or c) for each paragraph.

**Paragraph 1:** *________. First, you can meet people from different cultures. Also, you can try new food and learn about history. Finally, travelling helps you become more independent.*

- a) I went to Thailand last year.
- b) Travelling has many benefits.
- c) Travelling is expensive.

**Paragraph 2:** *________. For example, many teenagers spend more than five hours a day on their phones. This can cause eye problems and poor sleep. It can also make them feel stressed.*

- a) Smartphones are very popular.
- b) Too much screen time can be bad for young people's health.
- c) I have a new smartphone.

---

### Exercise 5: Put the Paragraph in Order

Put the sentences in the correct order (1–6).

- ___ a) For example, he helped me with my maths homework every evening.
- ___ b) The person I admire most is my older brother.
- ___ c) He is also very hard-working, so he got a scholarship to study abroad.
- ___ d) For these reasons, I hope I can be like him one day.
- ___ e) First, he is very patient and always helps me.
- ___ f) Now he works as an engineer in Singapore.

### Exercise 6: Find the Sentence That Doesn't Belong

Which sentence does **not** belong in the paragraph?

> **(1)** Learning English has many advantages. **(2)** First, it helps you get a better job because many companies need English speakers. **(3)** Second, you can communicate with people from all over the world. **(4)** My favourite food is fish amok. **(5)** Also, you can watch films and read books in English. **(6)** For these reasons, learning English is very useful.

### Exercise 7: Write a Paragraph (Guided)

Write a paragraph (80–100 words) on **one** topic. Follow the plan.

**Topic A:** *Why is exercise important?*
**Topic B:** *Describe your favourite place.*
**Topic C:** *Should children have a mobile phone?*

| Step | Your sentence |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. Topic sentence (main idea / opinion) | ________________ |
| 2. Reason 1 (*First,… because…*) | ________________ |
| 3. Example (*For example,…*) | ________________ |
| 4. Reason 2 (*Also, / In addition,…*) | ________________ |
| 5. Concluding sentence (*For these reasons,…*) | ________________ |

✔️ **Checklist:** topic sentence · 2 reasons · 1 example · 4+ linking words · concluding sentence

### Exercise 9: Write an Essay

Choose **one** question. Write **150–180 words** in **4 paragraphs**.

1. *Some people think students should not have homework. Do you agree?*
2. *Is it better to study alone or in a group?*
3. *Should people eat less meat? Give your opinion.*
4. *Is social media good or bad for teenagers?*

✔️ **Checklist:** clear opinion in the introduction · one reason per body paragraph · examples · different linking words · conclusion repeats the opinion in new words · 150–180 words

### Exercise 10: Write an Email

Choose **one** task. Write **100–120 words**.

**Task A (informal):** *Your friend is visiting your town next month. Write an email. Tell them: what places to visit, what food to try, and ask when they are arriving.*

**Task B (semi-formal):** *You want to join an English course at a language school. Write an email to the school. Ask about: the class times, the price, and the level test.*

✔️ **Checklist:** correct greeting · reason for writing · all 3 points answered · closing line · correct sign-off

## ✅ Answer Key

### Exercise 1
1. so · 2. because · 3. Although · 4. for example · 5. However · 6. but · 7. Then · 8. also

### Exercise 2
1. I stayed at home **because** it was very cold.
2. My brother, **who** works in Phnom Penh, is a doctor. / My brother is a doctor **who** works in Phnom Penh.
3. **Although** the film was long, I enjoyed it.
4. **If** you study every day, you will pass the exam.
5. **After** I finished my homework, I watched TV.
6. This is the café **where** we first met.

### Exercise 3
1. *Because I was hungry, I ate a sandwich.* (needs a main clause)
2. She **doesn't** like spicy food.
3. I **agree** with you.
4. Yesterday we **went** to the beach.
5. I went to the market**. It** was very busy. / *…, **and** it was very busy.*
6. Can you give me some **information**?
7. Everybody **has** a smartphone these days.
8. I **like reading books very much**. / I **really like** reading books.

### Exercise 4
1. **b** — the paragraph lists benefits of travelling.
2. **b** — the paragraph is about health problems from screen time.

### Exercise 5
**b → e → a → c → f → d**
> *The person I admire most is my older brother. First, he is very patient and always helps me. For example, he helped me with my maths homework every evening. He is also very hard-working, so he got a scholarship to study abroad. Now he works as an engineer in Singapore. For these reasons, I hope I can be like him one day.*

### Exercise 6
**Sentence (4)** — *My favourite food is fish amok.* It is not about the advantages of learning English.

### Exercise 7 — Model Answer (Topic A)
> Exercise is very important for both our body and our mind. **First,** it keeps us healthy **because** it makes our heart and muscles stronger. **For example,** people who walk for 30 minutes a day have a lower risk of heart disease. **In addition,** exercise helps us feel happier and less stressed. After a game of badminton, I always feel relaxed and sleep better. **For these reasons,** I believe everyone should try to exercise a few times a week. *(≈ 85 words)*

### Exercise 9 — Model Answer (Question 1)
> **Should Students Have Homework?**
>
> Many students complain about homework, and some people think schools should stop giving it. However, I do not agree. In my opinion, homework is useful if teachers give the right amount.
>
> Firstly, homework helps students remember what they learned in class. When they practise at home, the new information stays in their memory for longer. For example, I always do better in vocabulary tests when I review the words at home.
>
> Secondly, homework teaches students to be responsible and organised. They must plan their time and finish their work before the deadline. These are important skills for university and work. On the other hand, too much homework can make students tired and stressed, so teachers should be careful.
>
> To sum up, I believe homework is helpful because it improves memory and teaches good habits. However, it should not take up all of a student's free time.
>
> *(≈ 165 words)*

### Exercise 10 — Model Answer (Task B)
> **Subject:** Enquiry about English courses
>
> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to ask about the English courses at your school. I would like to improve my speaking and writing for my job.
>
> Could you please tell me what times the classes are? I work until 5 p.m. on weekdays, so evening or weekend classes would be best for me.
>
> I would also like to know the price of a three-month course, and whether books are included.
>
> Finally, do I need to take a level test before I start? If so, when can I take it?
>
> Thank you for your help. I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Kind regards,
> Vanna Sok
>
> *(≈ 115 words)*
