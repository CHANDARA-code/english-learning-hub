## 1. The exam task

| | IELTS Writing Task 2 |
| :-- | :-- |
| Time | About **40 minutes** (the whole Writing test is 60 minutes; Task 2 counts for twice as much as Task 1) |
| Length | **At least 250 words** — shorter answers lose marks |
| Assessed on | **Task Response** · **Coherence and Cohesion** · **Lexical Resource** · **Grammatical Range and Accuracy** (25% each) |

---

## 2. Levels and bands

Every model essay is written at one CEFR level. The IELTS band next to it is the commonly used CEFR ↔ IELTS alignment, shown as a guide only.

| Level | IELTS band | What the model essays at this level show |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 🟡 **B1** | 4–5 | 4–5 short paragraphs · clear opinion · basic linking words (*firstly, however, for example, in conclusion*) · simple and compound sentences · personal examples · everyday vocabulary |
| 🔴 **B2** | 5.5–6.5 | Clear thesis · PEEL paragraphs · both sides with a rebuttal · passive, conditionals and relative clauses · topic collocations · hedging (*may, tend to*) · real-world examples |
| 🟣 **C1** | 7–8 | Qualified thesis · concession + rebuttal · nominalisation · precise vocabulary · evidence and named examples · a stated implication in the conclusion |
| ⚫ **C2** | 8.5–9 | Reframes the question while still answering it · controlled rhythm (long and short sentences) · rhetorical devices used sparingly · precise connotation · a memorable final line |

> ⚠️ **About the bands:** the models are written without mistakes, so they show *range*, not a real candidate's script. A real band 4–5 answer usually contains more errors, and an examiner's score depends on all four criteria.

📖 Techniques for each level: [B1](../b1_writing_techniques.md) · [B2](../b2_writing_techniques.md) · [C1](../c1_writing_techniques.md) · [C2](../c2_writing_techniques.md)

## 3. How each topic is organised

Every one of the 100 topics has the same structure:

| Part | What you get |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Question** | The exam-style prompt, its **question type** and **theme** |
| **Useful vocabulary** | 6 collocations and key terms for the topic |
| **✍️ One paragraph, four levels** | The **same idea** written as one body paragraph at B1, B2, C1 and C2 — the fastest way to see how language changes from level to level |
| **📝 Model essays** | A full essay at **each** level (B1, B2, C1, C2). The heading shows the word count, and every essay is over 250 words |

---

## 4. How to study with this bank

1. **Choose a topic** and read **only the question**. Cover the answers.
2. **Plan for 5 minutes, write for 40.** Aim for 260–300 words.
3. **Compare** your essay with the model **at your level**, then with the model **one level above**.
4. **Collect language**: copy 5 useful phrases from the vocabulary line and the higher-level essay into your notebook.
5. **Upgrade one paragraph**: rewrite your weakest paragraph at the next level, using the "one paragraph, four levels" section as a guide.
6. **Check** your rewrite with the self-check list in the technique file for your level.

> 💡 **Tip:** do one topic from each question type before repeating a type. That way you practise every essay structure.

## 5. The five question types

| Type | How to recognise it | What your essay must do | Count | See an example |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Opinion** (agree / disagree) | *To what extent do you agree or disagree?* | Give a clear position in the introduction and keep it to the end | 38 | [Topic 1](ielts_01_education.md#1-free-university-education) |
| **Discussion** | *Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* | Explain **both** views fairly, then give **your** view | 27 | [Topic 4](ielts_01_education.md#4-single-sex-or-mixed-schools) |
| **Advantages and disadvantages** | *What are the advantages and disadvantages?* / *Do the advantages outweigh…?* | Cover both sides; for "outweigh", say clearly which side is stronger | 15 | [Topic 3](ielts_01_education.md#3-studying-abroad) |
| **Problem and solution** | *What are the causes / problems… and what can be done?* | Explain the causes or problems, then match a solution to each | 22 | [Topic 7](ielts_01_education.md#7-teenagers-leaving-school-early) |
| **Two-part question** | Two direct questions, e.g. *Why is this happening? Is it positive or negative?* | Answer **both** questions fully, usually one body paragraph for each | 18 | [Topic 9](ielts_01_education.md#9-taking-a-gap-year) |
