## Paragraph 1 — Working from Home (Opinion)

**Question:** *Do you think working from home is a good idea?*

> I think working from home is a good idea for many people. **First,** workers do not need to travel to the office, **so** they save time and money. **For example,** my sister used to spend two hours in traffic every day, **but** now she uses that time to exercise and cook. **Also,** people can work in a quiet place **and** focus better. **However,** some people feel lonely **because** they do not see their colleagues. **In my opinion,** the advantages are bigger than the disadvantages, **so** more companies should let their staff work from home.

### 🔎 Analysis
| Part | Technique |
| :--- | :--- |
| *I think working from home is a good idea…* | **Topic sentence** with opinion |
| *First,… so…* | Reason 1 + result |
| *For example, my sister…* | **Personal example** |
| *However, some people feel lonely…* | Shows the **other side** briefly |
| *In my opinion,… so…* | **Concluding sentence** repeats the opinion |
| Connectors | *first, so, for example, but, also, and, however, because* |
| Word count | ~95 words |

## Paragraph 2 — My Hometown (Descriptive)

> My hometown is Kampot, a small, quiet town in the south of Cambodia. It is famous **for** its river, its pepper, and the beautiful mountains **that** surround it. In the evening, many people walk along the riverside **and** watch the sunset. The weather is usually warm, **but** it is cooler than in Phnom Penh. The people are very friendly, **and** life is slower **than** in the city. **Although** there are not many big shops, there are lots of small cafés **where** you can relax. I love my hometown **because** it is peaceful **and** full of natural beauty.

### 🔎 Analysis
- **Adjectives** for description: *small, quiet, beautiful, warm, friendly, peaceful*
- **Relative clauses:** *…mountains **that** surround it / …cafés **where** you can relax*
- **Comparatives:** *cooler than, slower than*
- **Although**-clause to add contrast
- Ends with a **personal feeling**

## Paragraph 3 — A Day I Will Never Forget (Narrative)

> **Last year,** I took my first long bus trip alone. **At first,** I felt nervous **because** I had never travelled without my family. The bus **left** at six in the morning, **and** it **was raining** heavily. **While** I **was looking** out of the window, an old woman sat next to me **and** started talking. She told me funny stories about her life, **and** the time passed quickly. **Suddenly,** the bus stopped. There was a problem with the engine! We waited for two hours, **but** the woman shared her food with me. **In the end,** we arrived safely in Siem Reap. **I learned that** strangers can be very kind, **and** now I am not afraid to travel alone.

### 🔎 Analysis
- **Past simple** (actions) + **past continuous** (background): *was raining, was looking*
- **Past perfect** for earlier events: *I **had never travelled***
- **Time sequencers:** *last year, at first, while, suddenly, in the end*
- **Feelings:** *nervous, not afraid*
- **Lesson** at the end: *I learned that…*

## Essay — Online Learning vs Classroom Learning

**Question:** *Some people think that students learn better online than in a classroom. Do you agree? Write 150–180 words.*

> **Online Learning or Classroom Learning?**
>
> **¶1 (Introduction)** Nowadays, many students take online courses **instead of** going to school every day. **Some people** believe that online learning is better, **but I disagree.** **In my opinion,** students learn more in a classroom.
>
> **¶2 (Body 1)** **Firstly,** in a classroom students can ask the teacher questions and get answers immediately. **This is important because** they can understand difficult lessons more quickly. **For example,** when I studied maths online, I often waited a long time for the teacher to reply to my messages.
>
> **¶3 (Body 2)** **Secondly,** classrooms help students to make friends and work in teams. **When** students study together, they learn how to communicate and share ideas. **On the other hand,** online learning can be lonely, **and** some students lose motivation.
>
> **¶4 (Conclusion)** **To sum up,** online learning is useful and flexible, **but** I believe classroom learning is better **because** students get more help and have more contact with other people.

*(≈ 165 words)*

### 🔎 Analysis
| Paragraph | What it does | Key language |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¶1 | Background + **clear opinion** | *Nowadays… Some people believe… but I disagree.* |
| ¶2 | Reason 1 + reason why + **example** | *Firstly… This is important because… For example…* |
| ¶3 | Reason 2 + **other side** | *Secondly… When… On the other hand…* |
| ¶4 | Restates opinion **in new words** | *To sum up… but I believe… because…* |

## Informal Email — Inviting a Friend

**Task:** *Write an email to your friend inviting them to your birthday party. Say when and where it is, what you will do, and ask them to reply. (100–120 words)*

> **Subject:** My birthday party! 🎉
>
> Hi Linh,
>
> How are you? I hope your new job is going well!
>
> **I'm writing to invite you to** my birthday party next Saturday, 12 October. It **starts at** 6 p.m. at my house. My parents **are going to** cook a big dinner, **and** after that we're going to play games and sing karaoke. My cousins from Battambang are coming too, **so** it will be really fun.
>
> **Could you** bring your guitar? Everyone loves it when you play!
>
> **Please let me know** if you can come by Wednesday, **because** I need to buy enough food.
>
> **Can't wait to see you!**
>
> Love,
> Sophea

### 🔎 Analysis
- **Friendly opening:** *How are you? I hope…*
- **Reason for writing:** *I'm writing to invite you to…*
- **Future forms:** *is going to / are coming / will be*
- **Request:** *Could you…?*
- **Closing + sign-off:** *Can't wait to see you! / Love,*
- Contractions are OK in informal emails.

## Before & After: Improving a Weak Paragraph

### ❌ Before (A2 style)
> I like sport. Sport is good. I play football. Football is fun. I play with my friends. It is good for health. I like it very much.

**Problems:** no topic sentence, very short repeated sentences, no linking words, repeated words (*sport, good, like*), no example with detail.

### ✅ After (B1 style)
> Playing sport is one of my favourite free-time activities. I play football with my friends every Sunday afternoon **because** it helps me relax after a busy week. **In addition,** it keeps me fit and healthy. **For example,** since I started playing regularly, I have lost three kilos and I sleep much better. Football **also** teaches me teamwork, **which** is useful at work too. **For these reasons,** I think everyone should try to do some kind of sport.

**Improvements:** topic sentence ✔️ · connectors ✔️ · present perfect ✔️ · relative clause ✔️ · concrete example ✔️ · concluding sentence ✔️
