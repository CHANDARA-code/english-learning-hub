## Contextual Vocabulary Passage: The Revival of Traditional Crafts

**Passage Word Count:** 306 words  
**Lexical Focus:** Word families, everyday phrasal verbs, collocations (*do vs make vs take*), dependent prepositions  

> **¶1** In modern industrialized societies, machine-made goods dominate retail markets. However, in several historical towns across Southeast Asia, a growing movement of young artisans **has decided to take up** traditional handicraft production. Rather than seeking routine office employment in noisy capital cities, these passionate creators are returning to their ancestral villages to preserve cultural heritage. They specialize in weaving natural silk scarves, carving wooden kitchen utensils, and throwing ceramic pottery.
>
> **¶2** There are several compelling reasons for this creative shift. First, consumers are increasingly **interested in** purchasing environmentally sustainable products. Unlike plastic kitchenware **which causes** long-term ecological damage, handmade clay bowls are completely organic and biodegradable. In addition, customers are willing to **pay a higher price** for unique items that possess personal character. When someone buys a handmade silk scarf, they are not merely purchasing fabric; they are supporting a local artisan's family.
>
> **¶3** Running a small craft workshop requires enormous **dedication** and patience. Young apprentices must spend months learning how to handle delicate materials without making careless **mistakes**. Furthermore, organizers must **deal with** practical business hurdles, such as securing reliable supplies of raw cotton and marketing their products on digital platforms. Many cooperative ventures have successfully **set up** online stores to sell their products directly to international tourists.
>
> **¶4** Older villagers, who initially feared that their ancient skills would vanish forever, are now **proud of** the younger generation's accomplishments. Local community leaders agree that blending traditional techniques with modern e-commerce provides a viable economic future. Ultimately, this handicraft renaissance proves that ancient traditions do not have to **disappear** in the face of modern technological progress; rather, they can adapt, flourish, and enrich our everyday lives.

### Part 1: Multiple Choice Cloze (Items 1–5)

   A. take up  
   B. take off  
   C. take after  
   D. take down  

2. Many international tourists are very interested ________ purchasing authentic handmade silk.  
   A. on  
   B. at  
   C. in  
   D. for  

3. The young apprentice was determined not to ________ any mistakes during the weaving process.  
   A. do  
   B. make  
   C. take  
   D. have  

4. Handcrafted wooden plates are completely safe for the environment because they are ________.  
   A. biodegradable  
   B. artificial  
   C. temporary  
   D. careless  

5. Local families are extremely proud ________ their children's artistic accomplishments.  
   A. with  
   B. about  
   C. of  
   D. to

## Answer Key with Detailed Lexical Explanations

### Part 1 Solutions
1. **Answer: A (take up).** *Take up* means to begin a new hobby or pursuit. *Take off* means an aircraft leaving the ground or becoming successful; *take after* means resembling a parent.
2. **Answer: C (in).** The dependent preposition for the adjective *interested* is always *in* (*interested in something*).
3. **Answer: B (make).** The fixed collocation with *mistakes* is *make a mistake*, never *do a mistake*.
4. **Answer: A (biodegradable).** Organic materials that decompose naturally without pollution are *biodegradable*.
5. **Answer: C (of).** The dependent preposition for *proud* is *of* (*proud of someone/something*).

### Part 2 Solutions
6. **dedication** (Verb *dedicate* $\rightarrow$ abstract noun *dedication*).
7. **careless** (Noun *care* $\rightarrow$ negative adjective *careless* = not paying attention).
8. **development** (Verb *develop* $\rightarrow$ noun *development*).
9. **reliable** (Verb *rely* $\rightarrow$ adjective *reliable* = dependable).
10. **reappearance / appearance** (Noun derived from *appear*).

### Part 3 Solutions
11. **make** (*make a decision*).
12. **take** (*take a tour / take an excursion*).
13. **do** (*do business with someone*).
14. **take** (*take a break*).
15. **have** (*have a meeting / have a conversation*).

### Part 4 Solutions
16. **down** (*turn down* = reject / refuse).
17. **after** (*look after* = take care of / mentor).
18. **out of** (*run out of* = deplete supply / have none left).
19. **with** (*deal with* = handle or manage a challenge).
20. **up** (*set up* = establish or found an organization).
