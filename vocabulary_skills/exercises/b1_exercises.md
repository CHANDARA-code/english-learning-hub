## ✅ Answer Key with Lexical Explanations

### Section 1
1. **development** (Noun suffix *-ment*).
2. **courageous** (Adjective suffix *-ous*).
3. **performance** (Noun suffix *-ance*).
4. **patience** (Noun form: *patient $\rightarrow$ patience*).
5. **clearly** (Adverb modifying *spoke*).
6. **environmental** (Adjective modifying *damage*).
7. **practical** (Adjective modifying *solution*).
8. **determination** (Abstract noun suffix *-ation*).

### Section 2
9. **make** (*make the bed*).
10. **make** (*make an appointment*).
11. **do** (*do someone a favor*).
12. **take** (*take notes*).
13. **take / have** (*take a break / have a break*).
14. **make** (*make progress*).
15. **do** (*do wonders for something*).
16. **had** (*have a headache*).

### Section 3
17. **put off** (Postpone).
18. **turn down** (Refuse/reject).
19. **run out of** (Deplete supply).
20. **give up** (Stop a habit).
21. **look after** (Take care of).
22. **looking forward to** (Anticipate with pleasure).
23. **call off** (Cancel).
24. **find out** (Discover information).

### Section 4
25. **at** (*good at*).
26. **on** (*keen on*).
27. **for** (*famous for*).
28. **of** (*terrified of*).
29. **with** (*satisfied with*).
30. **from** (*different from*).
31. **in** (*interested in*).
32. **of** (*proud of*).

### Section 5
33. **exhausted** (Upgraded from *very tired*).
34. **beneficial** (Upgraded from *very good*).
35. **enormous** (Upgraded from *extremely big*).
36. **tiny** (Upgraded from *very small*).
37. **fascinating** (Upgraded from *very interesting*).
38. **delighted** (Upgraded from *very happy*).

### Section 6
39. **lend** (Lend = give temporarily; borrow = take temporarily).
40. **told** (*Tell* requires a personal object: *told me*).
41. **remind** (Remind = cause someone else to remember).
42. **lose** (Lose = verb; loose = adjective meaning not tight).
43. **effect** (Effect = noun; affect = verb).
44. **advice** (Advice = uncountable noun; advise = verb).
