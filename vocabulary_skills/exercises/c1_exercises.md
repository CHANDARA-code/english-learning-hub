## ✅ Answer Key with Analytical Lexical Commentary

### Section 1
1. **alleviate** (*Alleviate suffering/pain* = to make physical or mental distress less intense).
2. **curtail** (*Curtail liberties/rights* = to restrict or impose an artificial limit on an action).
3. **ephemeral** (*Ephemeral trends/popularity* = lasting for only a brief day or season).
4. **transient** (*Transient population/campsite* = passing through without remaining permanently).
5. **attenuated** (*Attenuate competitiveness/strength* = weakened or reduced in force over time).

### Section 2
6. **irreproachable** (Prefix *ir-* + *reproach* + suffix *-able* = completely free from fault or blame).
7. **indignation** (Root *indignant* $\rightarrow$ abstract noun *indignation* = righteous anger).
8. **imperishability** (Prefix *im-* + *perish* + suffix *-ability* = inability to decay or be destroyed).
9. **anachronistic** (Prefix *ana-* + *chron* + suffix *-istic* = belonging to an obsolete historical era).
10. **privatization** (Root *private* + suffix *-ization* = transferring state assets to private ownership).
11. **impermissible** (Prefix *im-* + *permit* + suffix *-ible* = forbidden; not permissible).
12. **disintegration** (Negative prefix *dis-* + root *integrate* + suffix *-ion* = crumbling into fragments).
