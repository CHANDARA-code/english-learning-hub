## Section 1: Tertiary Polysemic Discrimination

*In each sentence below, a familiar English word is used in an elevated, secondary, or tertiary sense. Identify the correct contextual definition:*

1. *"The regional governor would **brook** no interference with the municipal elections."*  
   👉 **brook** means:  
   A. Navigate a freshwater river  
   B. Tolerate or endure (used negatively)  
   C. Sponsor financially  

2. *"The dean felt compelled to strictly **qualify** her public endorsement of the new curriculum."*  
   👉 **qualify** means:  
   A. Pass an official examination  
   B. Add caveats, conditions, or limitations to  
   C. Celebrate enthusiastically  

3. *"The unexpected discovery of structural cracks in the fuselage **arrested** the airline's expansion plans."*  
   👉 **arrested** means:  
   A. Placed in legal custody  
   B. Halted or brought to a sudden stop  
   C. Attracted media praise  

4. *"After seven grueling rounds of debate, the parliamentary delegates' enthusiasm began to **flag**."*  
   👉 **flag** means:  
   A. Display national symbols  
   B. Decline in energy, vigor, or momentum  
   C. Escalate into violence  

5. *"No sovereign democracy could possibly **countenance** the extralegal execution of prisoners."*  
   👉 **countenance** means:  
   A. Stare directly at  
   B. Sanction, tolerate, or permit  
   C. Translate into legal documents

## ✅ Answer Key with Masterclass Epistemic Commentary

### Section 1
1. **Answer: B (Tolerate or endure).** The verb *to brook* is used in negative constructions meaning to refuse to tolerate or permit (*brook no interference*).
2. **Answer: B (Add caveats or limitations).** In academic registers, *qualify* means to make a statement less sweeping or absolute.
3. **Answer: B (Halted or brought to a stop).** Secondary mechanical/temporal sense of *arrest*.
4. **Answer: B (Decline in energy or momentum).** Vigor, enthusiasm, or alertness *flags* when it begins to fade.
5. **Answer: B (Sanction, tolerate, or permit).** Formal political/legal register for giving consent or approval.

### Section 2
6. **solipsism** (The philosophical belief that only one's own mind exists).
7. **hermeneutic** (Concerning the theory and methodology of interpretation).
8. **antediluvian** (Literally *before the flood*; ridiculously archaic or outmoded).
9. **panoply** (An impressive, extensive array or collection).
10. **teleology** (The doctrine that biological or cosmic design is directed toward an ultimate end).
11. **recalcitrant** (Obstinately defiant of authority).
12. **peripatetic** (Nomadic, wandering, or traveling from place to place).

### Section 3
13. **C (A Procrustean bed)** (Ruthless enforcement of arbitrary conformity).
14. **D (A Pyrrhic victory)** (A victory so costly it destroys the victor).
15. **A (A Faustian bargain)** (Surrendering moral integrity for power or wealth).
16. **B (A Gordian knot)** (An intricate problem solved through bold, decisive action).

### Section 4
17. **prima facie** (*at first sight*; evident on the face of it).
18. **ipso facto** (*by the very fact itself*).
19. **status quo ante** (The state of affairs existing prior to the conflict).
20. **quid pro quo** (A reciprocal concession or exchange).
21. **inter alia** (Among other things).

### Section 5
22. **eventual** (Noun *event* $\rightarrow$ adjective *eventual* = occurring at the end).
23. **undecaying** (Negative prefix *un-* + participle *decaying* = resistant to time/decay).
24. **unashamed** (Negative adjective *unashamed* = brazen and unapologetic).
25. **impenetrable** (Prefix *im-* + root *penetrate* + suffix *-able* = impossible to decode or enter).
26. **unprecedented** (Negative adjective = never having happened before).
27. **exceptionally** (Adverb modifying *rigorous*).

### Section 6
28. **Answer: A (assuage).** Standard collocational partner for *fears, anxieties, or panic*.
29. **Answer: A (threw down).** The fixed idiom is *throw down a challenge / throw down the gauntlet*.
30. **Answer: A (precipitated).** Formal academic verb meaning triggered or caused to occur suddenly.
31. **Answer: A (reconcile).** Collocation: *reconcile contradictions / reconcile conflicting models*.

### Section 7
32. **solipsism**
33. **dissonance**
34. **flag**
35. **immutable**
36. **circumscribed**
37. **ephemera**
