## Section 1: Advanced Word Formation (Cambridge FCE Part 3 Style)

1. The benefits of transitioning to renewable energy vastly ____________ the financial costs. **(WEIGH)**

8. The scientific community reached a general ____________ regarding the causes of climate change. **(CONSENT)**

## ✅ Answer Key with Analytical Annotations

### Section 1
1. **outweigh** (Prefix *out-* = exceed in value or importance).
2. **mismanagement** (Prefix *mis-* + noun *management*).
3. **vulnerability** (Adjective *vulnerable* $\rightarrow$ noun *vulnerability*).
4. **illogical** (Negative prefix *il-* before 'l').
5. **sustainable** (Suffix *-able* indicating capability).
6. **anxiety** (Adjective *anxious* $\rightarrow$ abstract noun *anxiety*).
7. **accessibility** (Suffix *-ability*).
8. **consensus** (Latin loan noun denoting collective agreement).

### Section 2
9. **D (allocate resources)**
10. **C (pose a threat)**
11. **A (conduct an investigation)**
12. **B (put forward a hypothesis)**
13. **E (spark controversy)**
14. **profound** (*profound impact*).
15. **pressing** (*pressing dilemma*).
16. **substantial** (*substantial contribution*).
17. **unprecedented** (*unprecedented growth*).
18. **viable** (*viable alternative*).

### Section 3
19. **put forward** (Proposed).
20. **take on** (Hire new staff).
21. **brought about** (Caused).
22. **turned out** (Concluded/developed).
23. **comes across** (Makes an impression).

### Section 4
24. **persistent** (Positive connotation of perseverance).
25. **stingy** (Negative connotation of refusing to spend money).
26. **nosy** (Negative connotation of intrusive curiosity).
27. **slender** (Aesthetic positive; *gaunt* implies sickly thinness).
28. **resolute** (Positive connotation of firm principle; *obstinate* is negative stubbornness).

### Section 5
29. **double-edged sword**
30. **touch and go**
31. **pivotal role**
32. **foregone conclusion**
33. **see the bigger picture**

### Section 6
34. **eliminate** (Replaces *get rid of*).
35. **ascertain** (Replaces *find out*).
36. **tolerate** (Replaces *put up with*).
37. **solicit** (Replaces *ask for*).
38. **deteriorate** (Replaces *get worse*).

### Section 7
39. **deduce**
40. **fluctuate**
41. **criteria**
42. **monitor**
43. **transition**
44. **formulate**
