| Level | CEFR Target | Exam Equivalent | Lexical Focus & Word Count | Daily 3,000 Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B1** | Intermediate (មធ្យម) | Cambridge PET / IELTS 4.5–5.0 | Word families, *do/make/take*, high-frequency phrasal verbs, dependent prepositions | **Words 1–750** |
| **B2** | Upper Intermediate (មធ្យមកម្រិតខ្ពស់) | Cambridge FCE / IELTS 5.5–6.5 | Latin & Greek affixes, academic collocations, polysemic verbs, Academic Word List (AWL) | **Words 751–1500** |

## 🚀 Recommended Study Routine

1. **Daily Active Recall (15–20 minutes):** Review 25 words from the target level in [`daily_3000/`](file:///Users/chandara-dgc/Documents/learn-english/vocabulary_skills/daily_3000/). Read the sentence, predict the masked word, verify with the answer key, and review the Khmer translation.
2. **Strategy Mastery (1 session/week):** Study the corresponding level guide in [`technique/`](file:///Users/chandara-dgc/Documents/learn-english/vocabulary_skills/technique/) to master affixation rules, collocation patterns, and polysemy.
3. **Timed Mock Exam (Bi-weekly):** Read the ~300-word passage in [`examples/`](file:///Users/chandara-dgc/Documents/learn-english/vocabulary_skills/examples/) under exam conditions and complete the 20-question test.
4. **Targeted Drills (Weekly):** Test your precision using the exercise sets in [`exercises/`](file:///Users/chandara-dgc/Documents/learn-english/vocabulary_skills/exercises/) and analyze all distractor explanations.
