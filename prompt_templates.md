# English Learning Prompt Templates

A structured collection of effective AI prompt templates for learning English idioms, expressions, and vocabulary, tailored with Khmer translations and practice exercises.

---

## 1. Batch / Step-by-Step Learning Prompts (Most Effective)

Because AI outputs have length limits, asking for 20–30 items at a time prevents cut-offs while maintaining high-quality exercises and accurate explanations.

### A. General Daily English
```markdown
Provide a list of 25 common English idioms and expressions used in daily conversation. For each expression, include:
1) English definition
2) Khmer translation (អត្ថន័យជាភាសាខ្មែរ)
3) An example sentence
4) A fill-in-the-blank practice exercise with an answer key at the end.
```

### B. Series / Multi-Part Curriculum
```markdown
I want to learn 100 common English expressions in sets of 20. Please start with Set 1 (1–20). For each idiom, provide its meaning, Khmer translation, and a fill-in-the-blank quiz sentence. Tell me when you are ready for me to ask for Set 2.
```

---

## 2. Topic-Specific Prompts

Targeting specific contexts makes vocabulary and expressions significantly easier to contextualize and retain.

### A. Workplace & Professional
```markdown
Give me 20 common English business idioms used in office meetings and emails. Include fill-in-the-blank practice sentences for each, along with Khmer translations and explanations.
```

### B. Casual Slang & Informal Speaking
```markdown
Give me 25 modern conversational expressions and slang used by native speakers every day. Provide Khmer translations and a fill-in-the-blank practice exercise for each one.
```

### C. Emotions & Reactions
```markdown
Create a 20-question fill-in-the-blank worksheet for English expressions that describe emotions (stress, happiness, excitement, anger). Include Khmer definitions and answers.
```

---

## 3. Quiz & Test-Prep Prompts

### A. Multiple Choice (Exam Style: IELTS / TOEFL)
```markdown
Create 15 multiple-choice quiz questions testing common English idioms, similar to IELTS or TOEFL style. Provide 4 answer options for each, along with Khmer explanations for why the correct answer fits.
```

### B. Flashcard / Two-Column Table Format
```markdown
List 30 high-frequency English expressions in a two-column table: Column 1 with the expression and fill-in-the-blank sentence, Column 2 with the Khmer translation and answer key.
```

---

## Tips for Best Results
- **Pacing**: Focus on 15–25 expressions per study session to ensure long-term retention.
- **Active Recall**: Always attempt the fill-in-the-blank or multiple-choice questions before checking the answer key.
- **Application**: Write your own personal sentence for at least 5 new expressions each day.
